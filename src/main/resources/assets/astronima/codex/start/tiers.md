Everything in this mod sits on one ladder. Each rung is gated by a **thing you cannot fake**: a
material you have to find, a machine you have to build, an instrument you have to be wearing.
Nothing is gated by an experience bar.

> If you are stuck, you are missing a rung — not a recipe.

The rungs below are the ones the game actually enforces. They come from the same table the build
checks every recipe against: **nothing on a rung can need an ingredient from a rung above it.**

## Salvage

*What you woke up with, and what the rock gives up to bare hands.*

You have air for minutes and nothing else. The whole rung is about not dying while you find rock
worth breaking. Nothing here is manufactured — the pry bar and the suit came off the wreck, and
everything else breaks off in your hands.

- **Gates you:** oxygen in your suit.
- **You get:** the hammer stone, mineral salts, the first ore you can carry.
- **Read:** [[start/breathing]], and [[start/moving]] before you jump anywhere

## Ore

*Crushing and sorting: freeing the mineral, then separating it from the waste.*

Rock becomes material in real separation steps rather than one smelter. The crusher and the magnet
are the two machines you can build out of raw stuff, and that is why they come first.

- **Gates you:** the ore crusher, and something to turn it.
- **You get:** crushed ore, the magnetic separator, iron-nickel grains, tailings.
- **The trap:** grinding finer is not always better. See [[ore/processing]].
- **The upkeep:** these are the first machines that go out of true as you use them, and the
  first thing the wrench is really for — [[ore/calibration]].

## Metal

*Forging, and the plate everything after this is built out of.*

The cold forge, and the first things worth making: rod, plate, a pickaxe that does not fall apart.
**This rung is where the habitat comes from** — not the other way round.

- **Gates you:** the cold forge, and vacuum to weld in.
- **You get:** hull plate, iron rod, meteoric tools, and every suit repair part.
- **Read:** [[start/tools]] for what forging quality actually buys you.

## Chemistry

*Retort, Mond process, fluidized bed: metal without a furnace.*

Getting metal out of rock by chemistry instead of heat — and every vessel it needs is built out of
the rung below, which is why it sits here rather than beside the crusher.

- **Gates you:** carbon monoxide, and a room you can hold at a temperature.
- **You get:** pure nickel, iron powder, titania, baked silicate.
- **Read:** [[ore/carbonyl]] and [[ore/fluidization]]

## Habitat

*Sealing a volume and keeping what is in it breathable.*

A sealed room with breathable air in it: the first thing that makes the asteroid survivable rather
than merely survived. All of it made of plate, which is why it is here and not at the start.

- **Gates you:** hull plate, and finding the leak.
- **You get:** bulkhead doors, the airlock, plumbing, the scrubber, pressure you can read.
- **The instrument:** the gas analyzer. Before it, a room is either fine or you are dead.
- **Read:** [[life/air]] and [[life/plumbing]]

## Power

*Making electricity, moving it, and not setting fire to the run.*

The rung this mod is really about. Power is not a number in a bar: it is a conductor with a length,
a cross-section and a temperature. Logic lives here too — small parts that mount in the wire layer
itself, so a circuit is something you plaster into a wall.

- **Gates you:** rod stock, and a conductor you have chosen.
- **You get:** the array, the cell, wire you route pixel by pixel, six gates, the circuit plate.
- **The instrument:** the diagnostic goggles.
- **Read:** [[electrical/sizing]], [[electrical/protection]] and [[electrical/logic]]

## Medical

*Microbiology: culture it, identify it, treat the thing you found.*

Illness with a cause you can find. Symptoms first, a name much later, and only if you do the work
to get one.

- **Gates you:** glassware, and the patience to wait eighteen hours for a plate.
- **You get:** the Petri dish, incubator, microscope, synthesiser and decontamination station.
- **The trap:** treating with the wrong thing breeds resistance.
- **Read:** [[medical/bench]] and [[medical/clean]]

## Beyond

Cryogenics, semiconductors, fission, orbit. Each is its own ladder, and each is gated by the rung
below it rather than by a research screen.
