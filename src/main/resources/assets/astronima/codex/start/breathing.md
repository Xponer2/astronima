Air is not one number. A room can be at full pressure and still kill you, because what keeps
you conscious is the **partial pressure of oxygen** — how much of that pressure is oxygen
rather than something else.

> 16 kPa of oxygen is the floor. Below it you have minutes, and you will not notice.

## What actually kills you

- **Hypoxia.** Not enough oxygen. Quiet, and the reason a full-pressure room can be fatal.
- **Carbon dioxide.** You make it, and in a sealed room it builds up faster than the oxygen
  runs out.
- **Decompression.** Going from pressure to vacuum too fast, which is what the airlock exists
  to stop you doing.

## What to do about it

- An **oxygen candle** buys a room time, not safety.
- A **scrubber** takes the carbon dioxide back out. Without one, a sealed room becomes
  unbreathable while still being full of air.
- The **gas analyzer** {item:astronima:gas_analyzer} is what turns all of this from guessing
  into reading. Get one early.
