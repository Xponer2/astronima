A person exhales about a litre and a half of water a day, and in a sealed habitat it has nowhere
to go. Left alone the room drifts to saturation: fog, then frost, then mould.

## The condenser

It takes only the humidity **above** what the room can hold, so a dry room yields nothing and a
crowded one fills bottles by itself. Twenty-eight moles fill a bottle — about half a litre.
Right-click to collect.

> The water you breathe out is the water you drink. That loop is the whole reason a habitat can
> be closed at all.

## The other two sources

- **Ice**, mined directly. The obvious one, and the one that runs out.
- **The retort**, which bakes water back out of hydrated silicate — see [[ore/isru]]. That is
  water from *rock*, and it is the source that does not run out.

A fuel cell also makes water while it makes power: [[power/sources]].
