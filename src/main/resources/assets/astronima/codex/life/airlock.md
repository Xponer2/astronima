An airlock is not a door. It is a small room you can bring to vacuum and back without taking the
habitat with it, and every part of it is a block you place and wire yourself.

> Nothing here is automatic because nothing here is guessed. You commission it: the game
> validates what you built, it does not build it for you.

## What it is made of

- Two **bulkhead doors**, one to the habitat and one to outside. They interlock: while one is
  open the other is held.
- A **pump** and a **tank**, to take the chamber's air somewhere rather than throw it away.
- A **controller**, which sequences them and names the first thing that is wrong.

## Why you need one at all

Going from pressure to vacuum too fast gives you the **bends** — nitrogen coming out of solution
in your blood. It is survivable and it is not pleasant, and the pre-breathe station is the
answer: time spent on oxygen before you step out.

Throwing the chamber's air away every cycle is the other cost. A pump and a tank turn that from
a loss into a transfer — see [[life/plumbing]].

## When the lamp is amber

The controller names the **first** terminal that is wrong rather than saying "something". Go and
look at the device it names. That is the whole design of the readout: an instrument that says
*which* is worth ten that say *whether*.
