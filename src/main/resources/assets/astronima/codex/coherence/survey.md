Quantum coherence is a fragile thing — a system holding more than one state at once, right up
until something disturbs it enough to force a choice. On Earth this needs a laboratory: a
pumped-down, shielded, cryogenically cold chamber, built at enormous cost to keep the world out.

The asteroid already has three of those four conditions for free. It is cold. It is airless. Under
enough rock, it is shielded from radiation and undisturbed by anything at all — until you show up
and start living there. Every convenience that makes a habitat livable is exactly the kind of
disturbance a real lab spends millions to exclude.

## Six ways to lose it

Coherence time (**τ**, tau) is how long a state survives before something knocks it out. Six
separate causes each set their own limit, and — because they act independently — the *shortest*
one always wins, the same way a chain breaks at its weakest link:

| Cause | What does it | Reachable today? |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermal** | heat — anything above absolute zero jostles things | yes, room temperature |
| **Vibration** | sound and mechanical noise — a running machine, footsteps | yes, real acoustics |
| **Radiation** | ionising radiation punching through | not yet — nothing in the game emits a dose |
| **Field** | stray electric and magnetic fields near live wire | not yet — nothing senses this yet |
| **Gas** | collisions with residual gas molecules | yes, real room pressure |
| **Observer** | *you*, standing there | yes — always |

A site at ordinary cabin pressure and room temperature, with nobody isolating anything, holds a
state for only tens of microseconds — long enough to prove the physics is real, nowhere near long
enough to do anything with. A properly isolated, pumped-down, cold, quiet volume can hold one for
whole seconds instead. That difference — six orders of magnitude — is the entire reason the later
stages of this tier are worth building.

## The one you can never isolate away

The **observer** term is not like the other five. It has no isolation stage, no upgrade, no
amount of shielding that helps, because the thing disturbing the state is a warm, breathing,
electromagnetically noisy object standing in the room: you. While a player is present, τ is
clamped to about a microsecond no matter how good everything else reads.

There is exactly one way around it: **leave**. Every real experiment this tier eventually builds
is started remotely and watched from outside — which is not a limitation bolted on for game
balance, it is the actual reason real coherence experiments are run that way.

## Reading a site

{item:astronima:coherence_meter} carries the model into the world. Hold it in either hand and walk
— a live readout appears with one bar per cause, **sorted longest first**, so whichever bar is
shortest is always the next thing worth fixing. A needle beside the bars tracks total τ, and its
own behaviour is a second reading: a needle that swings and overshoots before settling means the
site is *fluctuating*, not just short — a problem a single steady number would hide entirely.
Right-click for the same numbers in chat, no HUD required.

Radiation and field currently read "no limit" everywhere — not because those causes do not exist,
but because nothing in the game emits a dose or a stray field yet. That is named honestly on the
meter rather than left off it, the same way the spectrograph names targets it cannot reach yet.

**TO USE:** hold {item:astronima:coherence_meter} in either hand and walk into the rock. Deep,
cold, quiet, shielded volumes read long. A site right next to a corridor you will later run a pipe
past reads well today and will not tomorrow — the meter cannot see a machine that has not been
built yet, only the one that has.
