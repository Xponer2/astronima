The richest single spectrum this catalogue holds — H-alpha, both sodium lines, both calcium lines,
helium, and iron, all in one source close enough to read in daylight rather than through a
telescope at night.

## Why it has no place on the chart yet

Every other object here sits at a fixed, real direction the atlas can actually draw a node at
([[sky/object_orion_nebula]] and its neighbours). The Sun does not — it is not a fixed point on this
world's sky the way a deep-sky object is, and giving it a real placement is still open work. Its
spectrum is real and already modelled; only its position on the chart is missing.

## What it already teaches

Sodium and calcium contamination, and the discovery of helium itself in 1868 — decades before
anyone found it on Earth — are both told from the Sun's own spectrum on [[spectroscopy/lines]] and
on each of those lines' own pages.
