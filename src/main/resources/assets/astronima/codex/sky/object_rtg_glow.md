A radioisotope thermoelectric generator's own waste heat: a real, honest continuum with no lines of
its own at all — nothing sharp, because nothing here is being excited into a specific transition,
only glowing from raw temperature the way any hot object does.

## Why that absence is the point

A blank reading is not a broken one. Pointing an instrument at something this catalogue truthfully
has nothing to identify in it, and getting back a real "no lines found" rather than an invented one,
is the same honesty every other target here is held to.
