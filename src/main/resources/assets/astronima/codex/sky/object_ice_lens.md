Four billion years undisturbed and stone cold. Nothing to see here without lighting it first — no
temperature means no glow, and no glow means no spectrum at all, real or otherwise.

## Why it is in this catalogue

A cold, dark target is not a failure state for an instrument; it is a real, valid reading. Nothing
in this catalogue pretends a target has a spectrum it does not.
