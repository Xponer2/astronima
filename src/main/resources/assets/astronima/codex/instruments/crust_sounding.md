Astra leaves the core with one spectrum. On the way up it passes through kilometres of crust, and
different minerals really do absorb different parts of it — what reaches a sounder is not the
core's own spectrum, it is the core's spectrum minus whatever the rock between you and it actually
took out.

## Which means the sounder is reading you, not guessing

The astra reaching a real position is a readout of everything really beneath it. The mineral table
this reads from is the same one the ore tier already uses (`sim/ore/Mineral`) — real densities,
real formulas, no invented tokens — so a sounding over a metal-rich seam really does read
differently from one over plain chondrite, in exactly the band that seam's own metal owns.

## The alphabet

Nine real minerals, each its own band: kamacite, magnetite, troilite, pentlandite, serpentine,
olivine, carbonate, tholin and ilmenite. A tenth (ice) exists in the alphabet but nothing in this
world can produce a reading for it yet — named honestly rather than hidden. An eleventh —
**unaccounted** — is not a mineral at all: it is what a reading cannot explain, and it stays silent
almost everywhere. Dig seriously close to the core, and it stops being silent. That is the honest
first hint that something down there is not on this list at all.

## TO USE

Hold the sounder and right-click. The reading covers the 32 real blocks beneath your feet — read it
again after digging further down, or somewhere else entirely, since the column is genuinely local.
