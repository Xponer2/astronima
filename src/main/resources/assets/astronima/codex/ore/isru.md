Smelting needs a furnace, a furnace needs fuel, and fuel needs oxygen you cannot spare. Every
process on this rung gets metal out of rock **by chemistry instead**, and each is a real
industrial method that exists because somebody had the same problem.

## The solar retort — water out of rock

A mirror, open sky, and a temperature you set with the focus. Hydrated silicate gives up its
water when baked; chlorate gives up oxygen; push either too hot and you spoil it.

> Hotter stops being better partway along and then becomes actively worse. That is why the
> retort's gauge reads against a **window** rather than filling up like a bar — a bar that filled
> would be praising the mistake.

It needs sun, so it has the array's placement problem: roof it over and it does nothing. See
[[power/sources]].

## The carbonyl refiner — nickel that walks out as a gas

The **Mond process**, and it is genuine: carbon monoxide plus nickel makes nickel carbonyl, a
gas, at low temperature — and heating that gas puts the nickel back down, pure. Iron does the
same thing at a different temperature, which is exactly what makes it a *separation* rather than
a melt. The two bands, the corridor between them and why the carbon monoxide comes back:
[[ore/carbonyl]].

## The fluidized bed — iron out of ilmenite

Blow hydrogen up through a bed of crushed ilmenite fast enough that the powder behaves like a
liquid. Too slow and the bed packs to the wall; too fast and it blows out the exhaust. Between
those two is where the reduction happens, and it gives back iron powder and titania. Why it has to
spin at all, and why the good band moves when you change the grind: [[ore/fluidization]].

Everything here starts with something the crusher and the separator made: [[ore/processing]].
