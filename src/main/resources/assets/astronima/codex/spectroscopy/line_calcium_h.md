396.8 nm — the stronger of ionised calcium's two great lines, and one of the two ways solar activity
is actually tracked from the ground: how much these lines vary in strength over time traces a star's
own magnetic cycle.

## Where it shows up here

Andromeda's bulge and its companion M32 both carry it, part of the same real old-star absorption
forest their shared iron line sits inside. See [[spectroscopy/line_calcium_k]] for its own paired
line.
