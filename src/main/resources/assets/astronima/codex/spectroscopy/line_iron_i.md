527.0 nm — one line out of iron's own real forest of absorption lines, and the one this catalogue
tracks. A rock's iron content has been readable this way, without crushing or heating a single
sample, since the earliest days of stellar spectroscopy.

## Where it shows up here

Both Andromeda's old bulge and its companion M32 carry it — two unrelated ageing stellar populations
whose light is dominated by the same kind of cool, iron-rich giant stars the Sun itself is. Matching
this one line between the two, at a distance of two and a half million light-years, is the entire
case behind [[research/claim_metal_assay]].
