393.4 nm — ionised calcium's second great line, [[spectroscopy/line_calcium_h]]'s own pair, and
together the two are simply called "Ca II H and K" in real spectroscopy.

## Where it shows up here

Andromeda and M32 both carry it, the same real old-star signature their shared iron and sodium
lines belong to.
