589.0 nm — the shorter-wavelength half of sodium's doublet, [[spectroscopy/line_sodium_d1]]'s own
twin, 0.6 nm away. Both come from the identical sodium atom, split by a small quantum-mechanical
effect (the electron's own spin interacting with its orbit) rather than being two different
elements' lines that happen to sit close together.

## Where it shows up here

Andromeda and M32 both carry it, alongside D1 and the rest of their shared old-star absorption
suite.
